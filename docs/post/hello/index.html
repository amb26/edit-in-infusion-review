Hello from the review site – November 23, 2026

This post exists to be edited. Click the “Edit in Infusion” button in your browser’s toolbar, choose Edit this page, and change anything you like - this paragraph, the heading below, or the list. You can even change these words here! Or here.

Things to try

  • Change a word of this list, or add an item to it
  • Make some text bold or italic, or add a link
  • Unfold Edit as source in the panel to see your edit as markdown

Committing

Press Commit in the panel. The extension shows you the change in a window of its own, and once you confirm it, commits it to this site’s repository. The site is rebuilt in a minute or two, and the panel then shows your edit as published.
